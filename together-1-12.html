The whole course as one story

Think of it like this

Imagine learning to cook. First you learn your knives and heat (MLF's maths). Then you learn recipes (MLT's algorithms). Then you actually cook in a real kitchen with a timer (MLP's scikit-learn). Weeks 9 to 12 are the final course: dishes that need everything, like logistic regression, SVMs, ensembles and neural networks. Each of them is a recipe (MLT) built from an optimisation tool (MLF) that you run in code (MLP).

Tip

Exams covered. The End Term of each course covers all twelve weeks, so this page is the End Term plan for MLF, MLT and MLP together. It also contains both quizzes (weeks 1 to 4 and 5 to 8, boundaries inferred from past papers). Weeks 9 to 12 are the third block: the lessons are listed below, and weeks 5 to 8 and weeks 1 to 4 are the first two.

The idea

Notice the shifts. The MLP lanes are not in lock-step with MLT: MLP teaches logistic regression (W5) and SVM code (W8) before MLT's theory (W9 and W10), and MLP W9 to W10 (trees, forests) trail MLT W7 and W11. That is why the braided plans pair lessons by idea, not by week number.
