2. Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors

  • Concept: Eigenvalues are scalars associated with a linear transformation of a vector space, and eigenvectors are the corresponding vectors.
  • Equation: For a Hermitian matrix AAA, the eigenvalues λ\lambdaλ satisfy det⁡(A−λI)=0\det(A - \lambda I) = 0det(A−λI)=0.

3. Schur's Theorem

  • Concept: Every square matrix is unitarily similar to an upper triangular matrix.
  • Principle: The diagonal entries of the Schur form of a matrix are its eigenvalues.

4. Singular Value Decomposition (SVD)

  • Concept: Factorizing a matrix into three matrices UUU, Σ\SigmaΣ, and VTV^TVT.
  • Equation: A=UΣVTA = U \Sigma V^TA=UΣVT

  • Concept: An optimization algorithm used to minimize some function by iteratively moving in the direction of steepest descent.

7. Positive Definite Matrices

  • Concept: A matrix is positive definite if it satisfies xTAx>0x^T A x > 0xTAx>0 for all non-zero vectors xxx.
  • Principle: All singular values of a positive definite matrix are positive.

8. Unitary Matrices

  • Concept: A matrix UUU is unitary if U∗U=IU^* U = IU∗U=I.
  • Principle: The inverse of a unitary matrix is its conjugate transpose.
