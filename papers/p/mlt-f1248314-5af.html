4. Exam Tips

  1. SVM:

    • Hard-margin: No slack variables (ξi=0\xi_i = 0ξi​=0).
    • Soft-margin: ξi>0\xi_i > 0ξi​>0 for misclassified points.
    • αi>0\alpha_i > 0αi​>0 → Support vector; αi=C\alpha_i = Cαi​=C → Margin violator.
  2. PCA: Eigenvectors of CCC are principal components; eigenvalues are variances.

  3. Regularization:

    • λ↑\lambda \uparrowλ↑ → Underfitting (high bias).
    • λ↓\lambda \downarrowλ↓ → Overfitting (high variance).
  4. Decision Trees: Information gain measures purity (entropy reduction).
  5. Neural Networks: ReLU is common in hidden layers; sigmoid for binary output.
  6. Bayesian Learning: Beta prior + Binomial likelihood → Beta posterior.
