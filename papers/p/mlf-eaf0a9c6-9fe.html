Core Topics & Key Concepts

1. Linear Algebra & Matrix Theory

Key Concepts:

  • Quadratic Forms & Positive Definiteness

    • A quadratic form: f(x)=xTAxf(\mathbf{x}) = \mathbf{x}^T A \mathbf{x}f(x)=xTAx.
    • Positive Definite Matrix: xTAx>0\mathbf{x}^T A \mathbf{x} > 0xTAx>0 for all x≠0\mathbf{x} \neq 0x=0.
    • Eigenvalues: All eigenvalues of a positive definite matrix are positive.
    • Singular Matrix: det⁡(A)=0\det(A) = 0det(A)=0 ⇒ at least one eigenvalue is zero.
    • Symmetric Matrix: A=ATA = A^TA=AT.

  • Eigenvalues & Eigenvectors

    • For a matrix AAA, Av=λvA \mathbf{v} = \lambda \mathbf{v}Av=λv.
    • Unitary Matrix: A−1=A∗A^{-1} = A^*A−1=A∗ (conjugate transpose).
    • Hermitian Matrix: A=A∗A = A^*A=A∗.
    • Orthogonal Eigenvectors: Eigenvectors of a Hermitian matrix are orthogonal.
    • Eigenvalues of Unitary Matrices: Lie on the unit circle (magnitude 1).

  • Determinant & Rank

    • det⁡(A)=0\det(A) = 0det(A)=0 ⇒ singular matrix ⇒ at least one eigenvalue is zero.
    • Rank-Nullity Theorem: rank(A)+nullity(A)=n\text{rank}(A) + \text{nullity}(A) = nrank(A)+nullity(A)=n.
    • If det⁡(A)≠0\det(A) \neq 0det(A)=0, then nullity(A)=0\text{nullity}(A) = 0nullity(A)=0.
    • Non-zero eigenvalues of ATAA^T AATA and AATA A^TAAT: Same for any m×nm \times nm×n matrix AAA.

Key Concepts:

  • Convex Sets

    • A set SSS is convex if for any a,b∈Sa, b \in Sa,b∈S, θa+(1−θ)b∈S\theta a + (1-\theta)b \in Sθa+(1−θ)b∈S for θ∈[0,1]\theta \in [0,1]θ∈[0,1].

  • Convex Functions

    • A function fff is convex if:
f(θx+(1−θ)y)≤θf(x)+(1−θ)f(y)f(\theta x + (1-\theta)y) \leq \theta f(x) + (1-\theta) f(y)f(θx+(1−θ)y)≤θf(x)+(1−θ)f(y)

    • Necessary conditions for optimality in constrained optimization.

Exam Preparation Tips

  1. Linear Algebra:

    • Practice constructing matrices from quadratic forms.
    • Know how to check positive definiteness, eigenvalues, and singularity.

  1. Convex Optimization:

    • Understand convex sets, functions, and KKT conditions.
    • Solve small optimization problems using KKT.

  1. Probability:

    • Master transformations of random variables (Jacobian).
    • Know properties of Uniform and Normal distributions.

  1. Optimization Algorithms:

    • Be comfortable with gradient descent updates.
    • Understand constrained optimization (Lagrange multipliers).
