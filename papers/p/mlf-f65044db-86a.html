B. Linear Algebra & Matrix Decompositions

1. Singular Value Decomposition (SVD)

  • Key Idea: Decomposes a matrix AAA into A=UΣVTA = U \Sigma V^TA=UΣVT, where:

    • UUU: Left singular vectors (eigenvectors of AATAA^TAAT).
    • Σ\SigmaΣ: Diagonal matrix of singular values (square roots of eigenvalues of ATAA^T AATA).
    • VVV: Right singular vectors (eigenvectors of ATAA^T AATA).

  • Properties:

    • Rank of AAA = Number of non-zero singular values.
    • SVD is not unique for square matrices (depends on sign flips in UUU and VVV).

2. Principal Component Analysis (PCA)

  • Key Idea: Projects data onto a lower-dimensional subspace capturing maximum variance.

  • Steps:

    1. Compute the covariance matrix Σ\SigmaΣ.
    2. Find eigenvectors (principal components) and eigenvalues (variances).
    3. Sort eigenvectors by descending eigenvalues.

  • Properties:

    • Principal components are orthogonal and uncorrelated.
    • Directions of maximum variance = eigenvectors of Σ\SigmaΣ.
