1. Machine Learning Foundations

  • Classification vs. Regression:

    • Classification: Predict discrete labels (e.g., loan approval: Yes/No).

  • Vectors & Orthogonality:

D. Dimensionality Reduction

  • Autoencoders:

    • Encoder f:Rn→Rkf: \mathbb{R}^n \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^kf:Rn→Rk (compression).
    • Decoder g:Rk→Rng: \mathbb{R}^k \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^ng:Rk→Rn (reconstruction).
    • Loss: Mean squared error between input and reconstruction.

E. Basis & Row Space

  • Basis: Linearly independent vectors that span a space.
  • Row Space: Span of the rows of a matrix.
  • Rank: Dimension of the row/column space.

Exam Tips

  1. Polynomial Regression: Always use least squares to solve for θ\thetaθ (normal equations or gradient descent).
  2. Orthogonality/Projection: Check dot product for orthogonality; projection formula is critical.

  3. Eigenvalues:

    • Sum of columns = zero vector ⇒λ=0\Rightarrow \lambda = 0⇒λ=0.
    • Rotation matrices have complex eigenvalues (unless θ=0,π\theta = 0, \piθ=0,π).
  4. Linear Approximation: Compute gradient and evaluate at the point.
  5. Basis/Row Space: Row reduce the matrix to find basis vectors.
  6. Autoencoders: Reconstruction loss is MSE between input and output.
