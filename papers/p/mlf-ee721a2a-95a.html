4. Exam Tips

  1. Classification vs. Regression: Identify the problem type (discrete/continuous output).
  2. Polynomial Regression: Use least squares to solve for θ\thetaθ.

  3. Eigenvalues:

    • If all non-zero vectors are eigenvectors → A=λIA = \lambda IA=λI.
    • Sum of columns is zero → λ=0\lambda = 0λ=0 is an eigenvalue.
  4. Orthogonal Matrices: Rotation matrices are orthogonal but may lack real eigenvalues.
  5. Linear Approximation: Compute gradient and evaluate at the point.
  6. Basis: Ensure vectors are linearly independent and span the space.
