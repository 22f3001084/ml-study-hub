2. Unsupervised Learning

A. Clustering

  • K-Means:

    • Algorithm:

      1. Initialize centroids.
      2. Assign points to nearest centroid (Manhattan/Euclidean distance).
      3. Update centroids as mean of assigned points.

    • Silhouette Score (S):

      • S=b−amax⁡(a,b)S = \frac{b - a}{\max(a, b)}S=max(a,b)b−a​, where:

        • aaa: Mean intra-cluster distance.
        • bbb: Mean nearest-cluster distance.

      • Interpretation:

        • S≈1S \approx 1S≈1: Well-separated clusters.
        • S≈0S \approx 0S≈0: Overlapping clusters.
        • S<0S < 0S<0: Incorrect clustering (Q12).

3. Probabilistic Models

  • Naive Bayes:

    • Gaussian Naive Bayes:

      • Parameters: Mean (μ\muμ) and variance (σ2\sigma^2σ2) per feature per class.
      • Total Parameters: 2×num_features×num_classes2 \times \text{num\_features} \times \text{num\_classes}2×num_features×num_classes (Q21).
    • Assumption: Features are conditionally independent given the class.

4. Optimization

  • Gradient Descent:

    • Update Rule: w←w−α∇wL(w)w \leftarrow w - \alpha \nabla_w L(w)w←w−α∇w​L(w).
    • Learning Rate (α\alphaα): Affects convergence speed but not final solution for convex problems (Q3).
  • Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD): Mini-batch updates for efficiency.

5. Model Selection & Evaluation

  • Bias-Variance Tradeoff:

    • Underfitting: High bias (e.g., linear model on non-linear data).
    • Overfitting: High variance (e.g., high-degree polynomial).
  • Cross-Validation: Train/validation/test splits to evaluate generalization.

  • k-NN:

    • Hyperparameter (k): Larger kkk reduces noise but may underfit (Q4).

      • If k=10k=10k=10 has low training error but high validation error, increase kkk to smooth decision boundaries.

6. Multi-Label vs. Multi-Class

  • Multi-Class: Single label per sample (e.g., digit classification).
  • Multi-Label: Multiple labels per sample (e.g., predicting goals in 10 matches) (Q7).
