  • Loss Functions:

    • Squared Loss: L=∑(yi−f(xi))2L = \sum (y_i - f(x_i))^2L=∑(yi​−f(xi​))2.
    • Reconstruction Loss (Autoencoders): Mean squared distance between input and reconstructed input.

  • Unsupervised Learning:

    • Dimensionality Reduction (e.g., PCA, Autoencoders).

2. Linear Algebra

  • Vectors:

    • Orthogonality: Two vectors v1,v2v_1, v_2v1​,v2​ are orthogonal if v1Tv2=0v_1^T v_2 = 0v1T​v2​=0.
    • Projection of v1v_1v1​ onto v2v_2v2​:
projv2v1=(v1⋅v2v2⋅v2)v2\text{proj}_{v_2} v_1 = \left( \frac{v_1 \cdot v_2}{v_2 \cdot v_2} \right) v_2projv2​​v1​=(v2​⋅v2​v1​⋅v2​​)v2​
  • Parallel vectors: Scalar multiples of each other (v1=kv2v_1 = k v_2v1​=kv2​).

  • Matrices:

    • Eigenvalues/Eigenvectors:

      • Definition: Av=λvA v = \lambda vAv=λv.
      • Key Insight: If the sum of columns of AAA is the zero vector, λ=0\lambda = 0λ=0 is an eigenvalue.
      • Real symmetric matrices have real eigenvalues.

    • Diagonalizability:

      • A matrix is diagonalizable if it has nnn linearly independent eigenvectors (not necessarily distinct eigenvalues).
      • False Statement: "A matrix is diagonalizable iff it has nnn distinct eigenvalues."

    • Orthogonal Matrices:

      • Definition: ATA=IA^T A = IATA=I.
      • Example: Rotation matrix A=[cos⁡θ−sin⁡θsin⁡θcos⁡θ]A = \begin{bmatrix} \cos \theta & -\sin \theta \\ \sin \theta & \cos \theta \end{bmatrix}A=[cosθsinθ​−sinθcosθ​].
      • Properties: Preserves norms, eigenvalues lie on the unit circle (no real eigenvalues for 0<θ<180∘0 < \theta < 180^\circ0<θ<180∘).

    • Determinant:

      • For a matrix where every non-zero vector is an eigenvector with eigenvalue λ\lambdaλ, A=λIA = \lambda IA=λI.
      • det⁡(A)=λn\det(A) = \lambda^ndet(A)=λn (for n×nn \times nn×n matrix).

  • Row Space & Basis:

    • Row space of AAA: Span of its rows.
    • Basis: Linearly independent rows that span the row space.
    • Example: For A=[1023152−11]A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 3 & 1 & 5 \\ 2 & -1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}A=​132​01−1​251​​, a basis is {(1,0,2),(0,1,−1),(0,0,−1)}\{(1,0,2), (0,1,-1), (0,0,-1)\}{(1,0,2),(0,1,−1),(0,0,−1)}.

  • Linear Relations:

    • Example: R2=2R1+R3R_2 = 2R_1 + R_3R2​=2R1​+R3​ for rows of AAA.
