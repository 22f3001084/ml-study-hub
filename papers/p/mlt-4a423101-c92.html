1. Core Topics & Concepts

A. Clustering (K-Means & K-Means++)

Key Concepts:

  1. Lloyd’s Algorithm (Standard K-Means):

    • Iterative algorithm to minimize within-cluster sum of squares (WCSS).
    • Convergence: Always converges (monotonically decreases WCSS), but may converge to local optima.
    • Sensitive to outliers (centroids can be pulled toward outliers).
    • Not guaranteed to find the global optimum (depends on initialization).

  1. K-Means++ Initialization:

    • Improves initialization by spreading out initial centroids.
    • Probability of selection for next centroid:
P(x)=S(x)2∑x′S(x′)2,S(x)=min⁡j∥x−μj∥2P(x) = \frac{S(x)^2}{\sum_{x'} S(x')^2}, \quad S(x) = \min_{j} \|x - \mu_j\|^2P(x)=∑x′​S(x′)2S(x)2​,S(x)=jmin​∥x−μj​∥2
  • Tie-breaking affects final clusters if points are equidistant.

  1. Convergence in One Iteration:

    • If centroids are already at the mean of their clusters, the algorithm terminates in one step.

  • Within-Cluster Sum of Squares (WCSS):

B. Principal Component Analysis (PCA)

Key Concepts:

  1. Dimensionality Reduction:

    • Projects data into a lower-dimensional subspace while maximizing variance.
    • If data lies in a k-dimensional subspace, the first k principal components capture all variance.

  1. Eigenvalues & Variance:

    • Non-zero eigenvalues of the covariance matrix correspond to dimensions with variance.
    • Proportion of variance explained by the iii-th PC:

  1. Covariance Matrix:

    • For centered data X∈Rn×dX \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times d}X∈Rn×d, covariance is:

C. Bayesian Statistics (Beta Distribution)

Key Concepts:

  1. Beta Distribution as Prior:

    • Used for modeling proportions (θ∈[0,1]\theta \in [0,1]θ∈[0,1]).
    • Prior: θ∼Beta(a,b)\theta \sim \text{Beta}(a, b)θ∼Beta(a,b)
    • Posterior: θ∣data∼Beta(a+successes,b+failures)\theta | \text{data} \sim \text{Beta}(a + \text{successes}, b + \text{failures})θ∣data∼Beta(a+successes,b+failures)

4. Exam Tips

  1. PCA:

    • If data lies in a k-dimensional subspace, the first k PCs capture all variance.
    • Reconstruction error = 0 if k=rank(X)k = \text{rank}(X)k=rank(X).
    • Eigenvalues of XTXX^T XXTX give variance along PCs.

  1. K-Means:

    • Converges always, but may not find the global optimum.
    • K-Means++ improves initialization by weighting points by S(x)2S(x)^2S(x)2.

  1. Bayesian Estimation:

    • Beta(1,1) = Uniform prior.
    • Posterior mean = (a+successes)/(a+b+n)(a + \text{successes}) / (a + b + n)(a+successes)/(a+b+n).

  1. GMM/EM:

    • Likelihood involves a sum over components (mixture).
    • Responsibilities (γik\gamma_{ik}γik​) are soft assignments.

  1. Kernels:

    • RBF, Polynomial, Laplacian are valid.
    • ∣x−y∣|x - y|∣x−y∣ is invalid (not a kernel).
