1. Optimization and Critical Points

  • Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors: Properties of eigenvalues and eigenvectors, especially for matrices like AATAA^TAAT and ATAA^T AATA.

  • Principal Component Analysis (PCA): Projection of data onto lower-dimensional spaces using PCA, involving the computation of eigenvalues and eigenvectors of the covariance matrix.

3. Matrix Properties

  • Hermitian and Unitary Matrices: Properties and identification of Hermitian and unitary matrices.
  • Positive Definite Matrices: Identification and properties of positive definite matrices.

  • Algorithm: Iterative method to minimize a function using the gradient and learning rate ηt\eta_tηt​.
