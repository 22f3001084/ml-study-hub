2. Linear Algebra

Core Topics:

  • Matrix Properties

    • Orthogonal matrices: ATA=IA^T A = IATA=I.
    • Symmetric matrices: A=ATA = A^TA=AT.
    • Unitary matrices: AHA=IA^H A = IAHA=I (complex analog of orthogonal).
    • Hermitian matrices: A=AHA = A^HA=AH.

  • Rank & Eigenvalues

    • AAA and ATAA^T AATA share singular values.
    • AAA and ATAA^T AATA have the same non-zero eigenvalues.

  • Systems of Linear Equations

    • Condition for solvability: rank(A)=rank([A∣b])\text{rank}(A) = \text{rank}([A|b])rank(A)=rank([A∣b]).

  • Principal Component Analysis (PCA)

    • First PC: Eigenvector of the largest eigenvalue of the covariance matrix.
    • Maximizes variance in the data.
