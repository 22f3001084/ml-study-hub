Core Topics & Key Concepts

1. Linear Algebra & Matrix Theory

  • Quadratic Forms & Matrices:

    • Quadratic form: f(x)=xTAxf(\mathbf{x}) = \mathbf{x}^T A \mathbf{x}f(x)=xTAx.
    • Positive Definite Matrix: xTAx>0\mathbf{x}^T A \mathbf{x} > 0xTAx>0 for all x≠0\mathbf{x} \neq 0x=0.
    • Singular Matrix: det⁡(A)=0\det(A) = 0det(A)=0 ⇒ at least one eigenvalue is 0.
    • Symmetric Matrix: A=ATA = A^TA=AT.

    • Eigenvalues/Eigenvectors:

      • For symmetric matrices, eigenvalues are real, and eigenvectors are orthogonal.
      • AAA is unitary if A∗A=IA^* A = IA∗A=I (for complex matrices).
      • AAA is Hermitian if A=A∗A = A^*A=A∗.

  • Key Equations:

    • Eigenvalue equation: Av=λvA \mathbf{v} = \lambda \mathbf{v}Av=λv.
    • Determinant properties: det⁡(AB)=det⁡(A)det⁡(B)\det(AB) = \det(A)\det(B)det(AB)=det(A)det(B), det⁡(ATA)=det⁡(AAT)\det(A^T A) = \det(A A^T)det(ATA)=det(AAT).
    • Rank-nullity theorem: rank(A)+nullity(A)=n\text{rank}(A) + \text{nullity}(A) = nrank(A)+nullity(A)=n.

Exam Tips

  1. Matrix Properties: Focus on positive definiteness, eigenvalues, and symmetry.
  2. Convexity: Remember tests for convex functions (Hessian, composition rules).
  3. KKT Conditions: Memorize the 4 conditions and when λi>0\lambda_i > 0λi​>0.
  4. Probability: Practice transformations (e.g., U=X+YU = X + YU=X+Y, V=X−YV = X - YV=X−Y) and order statistics.
  5. Gradient Descent: Compute gradients carefully and apply the update rule step-by-step.
