Study the three courses together

MLF is the toolbox, MLT is the algorithms, MLP is the code. These four plans weave them in the best order: each idea comes just after the one it needs. Pick the block you are preparing for.

Quiz 1 of all three coursesWeeks 1-4 togetherFoundations: vectors, calculus, projection, eigenvalues, with PCA, kernel PCA, k-means, MLE and EM.Quiz 2 of all three coursesWeeks 5-8 togetherPrediction: symmetric matrices, SVD, gradient descent, convexity, with regression, ridge/LASSO, k-NN, trees and Naive Bayes, plus the MLP code.Quiz 1 and Quiz 2, half the End TermWeeks 1-8 togetherThe whole first half as one two-act story with a three-lane map, one order and three problems that cross it.End Term of all three coursesWeeks 1-12 togetherThe full course: every big idea traced through MLF, MLT and MLP, the weeks 9-12 plan, and the End Term map.
